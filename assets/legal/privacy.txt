# What Solenn collects

Solenn does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Calculations are kept in memory while the app is open and are discarded when it is closed.

# Network access

Solenn does not connect to the internet and does not request any permission on your device.

# Payment information

Solenn never asks for, stores or transmits payment card details.

# Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version ships with the app.
